Praelo

Terms

Praelo is an Android teleprompter. You record in the app, or insert a clip from the gallery. The file stays on the phone.

The app is free to install. The free script stops at 400 characters. The free version has a watermark, ads, and no burned-in subtitles. Premium removes those limits.

Praelo lists silence, a repeated phrase, and hesitation sounds. You can keep any row. The cut is made once, when you save. It does not replace an editor for a long film.

Install and payment happen on Google Play.
